Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

RideArrivo is operated by RICHATHAOIR LIMITED (CAC RC 1654710), which is the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy covers ridearrivo.com and the RideArrivo rider and driver apps. It sets out what we collect, why we collect it, the lawful basis we rely on, who else sees it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do about it, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and the transport rules we operate under in Lagos State.

1. Who we are and how to reach us

  • Data controller: RICHATHAOIR LIMITED, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under RC 1654710.
  • Registered office: Babajide Sanwo-Olu International Market, Ikosi, Lagos, Nigeria
  • Data Protection Officer: privacy@ridearrivo.com
  • General enquiries: info@ridearrivo.com

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, preferred language, country of residence, date of birth, and a hashed password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Identity documents: a photograph of a government ID or a passport number where we need to confirm who you are. For drivers, this also covers driver's licence number, LASDRI number, insurance details, vehicle documents and photographs, and the vehicle owner's name and contact details.
  • Trip and booking data: pickup and drop-off addresses and coordinates, additional stops, flight number if you give one, luggage details, vehicle preferences and accessibility choices, trip history, fares and payment references.
  • Location data: precise GPS location from riders and drivers during an active trip, and from drivers whenever they are online and available for work. We do not collect a driver's location while they are offline.
  • Safety data: the emergency contact name and number you choose to add, Emergency Button alerts you raise, route-deviation events generated during a trip, and, only after the Emergency Button has been pressed, audio from the in-vehicle listening device (see section 12).
  • Communications: messages and calls between rider and driver placed through the app, support tickets you open, and the operational emails and WhatsApp messages we send you.
  • Device and technical data: device type and operating system, app version, a push notification token, and the IP address your requests arrive from, which our servers process in order to deliver responses and to block abuse.

What we do not collect. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers in the apps or on this website, we do not build advertising or marketing profiles, and we do not sell personal data.

4. Automated processing and automated decisions

Some parts of the service run automatically. We think it is clearer to say exactly which, and what each one does:

  • Rides are not allocated to you by an algorithm. A booking is offered to drivers who are online, and a human driver decides whether to accept it.
  • Fares are calculated automatically from vehicle class, booking type, distance and duration. The price is shown to you before you confirm.
  • Vehicle class is suggested automatically from the luggage you enter. You can override the suggestion.
  • Route deviation is monitored automatically during a trip by comparing the vehicle's position and direction against the expected route. If something looks wrong it raises an alert for a person to review. The check does not by itself penalise a driver or a rider.
  • Identity and driver verification is decided by a person on our team reviewing the documents. It is not automated, and we do not use an external background-check or credit-scoring provider.

We do not make decisions that produce legal effects concerning you, or similarly significant effects, by solely automated means, and we do not carry out profiling for advertising. If that changes, we will update this section before it starts, and you will be able to ask for a human to review the decision, to put your point of view, and to contest it.

6. Storage, security and international transfers

  • Security: data in transit is protected with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Access to production data is limited to the people who need it for their work, sign-in tokens expire, and changes made through our internal tools are restricted to administrator accounts.
  • Where your data is processed: we serve Nigeria, but the providers listed in section 5 operate mainly in the United States, with flight lookups and the storage of our public forms handled in the European Union. Your data is therefore transferred outside Nigeria.
  • The basis for those transfers: under sections 41 to 43 of the NDPA we may send personal data outside Nigeria only on a recognised basis, and for each provider we check which basis applies and record that assessment. Where we rely on contractual clauses, we use them only after assessing that the safeguards they give are adequate for that provider and that transfer. Ordinary vendor terms are not treated as meeting the NDPA on their own. We rely on the transfer being necessary for a contract only where it is genuinely needed to deliver the trip you booked, for example a map lookup or a payment. If neither basis applies to a provider or a purpose, we will ask for your consent before transferring.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This website sets no cookies, and it runs no analytics, advertising or tracking technologies. It stores three things in your browser's local storage, all of which are needed for the site to work:

  • your sign-in token, so you stay signed in;
  • your language choice; and
  • the state of a safety feature while one is running, so that it survives a page reload.

Clearing your site data removes all three and signs you out. Because none of it is used for analytics, advertising or tracking, no consent banner is required for it.

The apps store a sign-in token on the device and hold a push notification token so we can send you trip updates. They contain no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

The one third party your browser contacts on these pages is Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to serve the typefaces.

Our public forms (forms.ridearrivo.com and the booking, membership, move, boat, air and easybook subdomains) work differently. They show a banner asking whether you accept analytics. Until you choose Accept, nothing from Google loads and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, we load Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to count visits and how far people get through each form. We do not send the answers you type into a form, such as your name, email address or phone number. You can change your choice at any time with the Analytics preferences link on those pages, and withdrawing stops further analytics.

If we ever add any other non-essential analytics or tracking, we will ask for your consent before it runs and give you a way to withdraw that consent.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to do any of the following.

  • Access: confirm whether we hold data about you, and give you a copy of it.
  • Correct: fix anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase: delete your data. You can delete your account yourself in the app under Profile, or by emailing us. Erasure is subject to the records we are legally required to keep, listed in section 8, including tax, accounting, fraud-prevention and dispute-resolution obligations. Anything we are not required to retain is deleted or anonymised.
  • Restrict: pause our processing while a dispute about the accuracy of your data, or about the grounds we rely on, is being resolved.
  • Object: object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interest.
  • Port: receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and have us send it to another provider where that is technically feasible.
  • Withdraw consent: for anything we do on the basis of consent, such as marketing email, withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that already happened lawfully, and it does not affect anything we do on a different basis.
  • Automated decisions: ask for a human to review any decision made about you by solely automated means, put your point of view, and contest the decision. As set out in section 4, we do not currently make decisions of that kind.

Email privacy@ridearrivo.com to use any of these. We will respond within 30 days, and there is no charge. We may need to confirm your identity first, so that we do not hand your data to somebody else.

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled it, you can lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng.

10. Minors

RideArrivo services are strictly intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email privacy@ridearrivo.com and we will remove it.

12. Emergency Button and in-vehicle listening device

The vehicles used for RideArrivo trips carry a listening device that can pick up audio inside the vehicle. It is not running during ordinary trips. It is activated only as a result of someone pressing the Emergency Button, as explained here.

  • Emergency use only. The Emergency Button, in the rider and driver apps and on this website, must only be used when an emergency occurs, meaning you or someone else in the vehicle faces a real threat to safety. It is not for complaints, disputes about fares or routes, or testing. Misuse can lead to suspension of your account.
  • What happens when it is pressed. The button first asks you to confirm and tells you that the listening device may be activated. Once you confirm, an alert goes to the RideArrivo operations team with your ride details and location. The operations team then activate the listening device so they can hear what is happening and decide how to respond, which may include calling you, your emergency contact, or the emergency services.
  • Purpose. The audio is used only to respond to the emergency and to investigate it afterwards. We do not use it for advertising, for profiling, or to monitor ordinary trips.
  • Who can access it. Only authorised members of our operations team who need it to respond or investigate. Access is restricted by account permissions, and sign-in tokens expire.
  • Who it is disclosed to. Not to other riders, drivers or vehicle owners. We share it with the police, the emergency services or another authority only where needed to deal with the emergency or where a valid court order, subpoena or legally binding mandate requires it (section 5). The providers that host our systems store it on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • How long we keep it. Only as long as needed to respond and to deal with any resulting investigation, insurance claim or legal proceeding, then it is deleted (section 8).
  • Security. Audio and the alert records are protected in transit with HTTPS and covered by the access controls in section 6.
  • Notice to everyone concerned. We tell riders, drivers and vehicle owners about the listening device in this policy, in the Terms of Service, in the driver agreement and in the vehicle owner agreement. Your rights in section 9 apply to this data.

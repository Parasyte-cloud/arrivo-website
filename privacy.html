Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

At RideArrivo, operated by RICHATHAOIR LIMITED (CAC RC 1654710), we value your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and your rights when using our website and mobile applications under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and relevant Lagos State transport laws.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, preferred language, and hashed passwords. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Trip and booking data: pickup and drop-off locations, flight numbers, luggage details, trip history, and fare details.
  • Driver and vehicle details: driver's license numbers, LASDRI numbers, vehicle registration and insurance documents, and owner payout details.
  • Automated data and cookies: device information (IP address, operating system, unique device IDs), app usage analytics, and web cookies used to optimize site performance and security.
  • Location data: precise GPS location collected from riders and drivers during active trips, and from drivers while online in the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To match riders with drivers and provide ride-hailing services.
  • To verify driver identities, vehicle eligibility, and perform background compliance checks.
  • To process payments securely via third-party payment gateways.
  • To send operational communications (ride status, receipts, account updates) and critical safety alerts.
  • To fulfill tax obligations and comply with transport regulations in Lagos State.

3. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only under these circumstances:

  • Trip completion: matching riders and drivers see essential names, contact options, and trip locations necessary for pickup and routing.
  • Service providers: payment processors (for example Paystack, for secure transactions) and flight tracking APIs (for example AviationStack, for flight status).
  • Law enforcement and legal requests: we will disclose user or GPS location data to police, government bodies, or legal authorities only when presented with a valid court order, subpoena, or legally binding mandate under Nigerian law, or in emergency situations involving immediate physical safety threats.

4. Data storage, security and international transfers

  • Security measures: all data in transit is protected using HTTPS/SSL encryption. System access is restricted on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Cross-border transfers: your data may be stored or processed on secure cloud servers hosted outside Nigeria. Any international transfer complies with NDPA safeguards to ensure your data receives equivalent protection.

5. Data retention

  • Active accounts: we retain your profile and trip records while your account remains active.
  • Closed accounts: following account deletion, we retain minimal transactional records for up to 7 years to comply with statutory tax, legal accounting, and dispute-resolution requirements, after which data is permanently erased.

6. Your data rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access or request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct inaccuracies in your personal details.
  • Request the complete deletion of your account and data.
  • Object to or restrict certain types of data processing.

To exercise any of these rights or contact our Data Protection Officer, email privacy@ridearrivo.com. You also reserve the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Minors

RideArrivo services are strictly intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Revisions will be indicated by an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of our app or website after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or to reach our Data Protection Officer, email privacy@ridearrivo.com. For anything else, email info@ridearrivo.com.

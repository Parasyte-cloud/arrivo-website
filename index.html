The process

Four steps, start to finish

  1. 01

    Pick your language

    Choose English or Français from the start — the whole booking flow follows in whichever you're most comfortable with.

  2. 02

    Enter your flight

    Tell us your flight number — we track it, so your pickup adjusts automatically if you're early or delayed.

  3. 03

    Tell us your luggage

    Check in how many bags and how big — we match you to a sedan, SUV, or truck that actually fits everything.

  4. 04

    Get matched

    A verified driver and the right-sized vehicle are assigned — you see their name, photo, and ID before they arrive.

  5. 05

    Track and share

    Watch your ride live on the map, and share the trip with someone who wants to know you're safe.

Built for three kinds of people

Who Arrivo is for

🧳

Riders & travellers

Airport pickups, everyday rides, multi-stop trips, and on-demand chauffeur booking — matched to a vehicle sized for your luggage, in the language you're most comfortable in.

🚙

Vehicle owners

Have a car, SUV, or truck sitting idle? List it for pickups, set your own availability, and get paid weekly or monthly — without driving it yourself.

🪪

Drivers

Flexible, app-based work with transparent earnings. Get verified once, then accept the rides that fit your schedule.

Trust, built in — not bolted on

Safety isn't a feature.
It's the whole design.

Every part of Arrivo was built around one question: would you feel comfortable if this were someone you love landing alone at night?

  • Verified identity, every time

    See your driver's name, photo, and verification status before they ever arrive.

  • Live tracking, not a promise

    Watch the actual route on a live map from pickup to drop-off.

  • Share your ride

    Send live trip details to someone you trust, in one tap.

  • Vetted vehicles & owners

    Every vehicle in the network is tied to a documented, agreed vehicle-owner relationship — not an anonymous car.

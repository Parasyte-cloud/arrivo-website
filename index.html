Before you book

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number, so your driver is there when you actually land.

How do I pay?

At checkout by card through Paystack, from your RideArrivo wallet balance, or with a membership.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel at least 48 hours before pickup for a full refund.

How far ahead should I book?

Airport pickups work best booked ahead. Need a ride sooner? Use ArrivoExpress.

Are the drivers verified?

Every driver completes identity and licence verification before accepting rides.

Is RideArrivo available in my language?

Yes. The website and booking are available in seven languages.

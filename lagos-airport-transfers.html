Lagos airport transfers, timed to your flight

Book a pickup or drop-off at Murtala Muhammed International (MMIA) or MMA2. We track your flight so your driver is there when you land, not when the schedule said you would.

Book an airport transfer

What you get

A driver who has your flight number and date, a fare you see before you confirm, and a way to share your trip with the person waiting for you. Airport pickups work best booked ahead so a driver is lined up for your arrival.

If your flight is delayed we follow the new landing time. If it is cancelled or badly delayed, contact us and we will sort it out with you.

Going to the airport

Book your drop-off for the time you need to be at departures. Tell us your flight number and we plan the pickup around it, including the Lagos traffic you would rather not gamble with.

Paying and cancelling

Pay at checkout by card through Paystack, from your RideArrivo wallet or with a membership. Cancel at least 48 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later than that, you get 50% back. Refunds are processed within 7 business days.

Need something on the day?

For a ride sooner than you could book ahead, use ArrivoExpress. For hotels, companies and event organisers, see corporate transport in Lagos.

Questions

What if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number, so your driver is there when you actually land. If it is cancelled or badly delayed, contact us and we will sort it out.

Which airports do you cover?

Lagos only for now: Murtala Muhammed International (MMIA) and MMA2.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Airport pickups work best booked ahead so a driver is lined up for your flight.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel at least 48 hours before pickup for a full refund. Later than that, you get 50% back.

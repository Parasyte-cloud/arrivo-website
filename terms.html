1. What RideArrivo is

RideArrivo connects riders with independent drivers and vehicle owners for airport pickups, on-demand rides, and chauffeur bookings in Lagos, Nigeria. RideArrivo is a technology platform. Drivers and vehicle owners are independent contractors, not RideArrivo employees.

3. Bookings, fares & payment

  • Fares are shown before you confirm a ride and are calculated based on vehicle type, distance, and luggage/vehicle-size requirements.
  • Payment is processed through Paystack. By booking a ride, you authorize the charge shown at checkout.
  • Fares may include applicable taxes, including the Lagos State transportation service tax.

4. Cancellations & Refunds

4.1 Standard cancellation policy

  • Cancel at least 48 hours before your scheduled pickup time: full refund.
  • Cancel less than 48 hours before your scheduled pickup time: 50% refund of the amount paid.

4.2 Confirmation calls before your trip

Before your scheduled pickup, RideArrivo will make two attempts to reach you (by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or email) to confirm your booking and pickup details.

  • If you tell us during either confirmation attempt that you want to cancel, and you do so within 6 hours after the second attempt, you'll receive a full refund.
  • If you don't communicate a cancellation within that 6-hour window and cancel afterward, you'll receive a 50% refund.

4.3 Refund processing time

Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days of the cancellation being confirmed. The exact time the money appears in your account depends on your bank or card issuer.

4.4 No-shows

If you're not at the agreed pickup location at the scheduled time and can't be reached using the contact details you provided, this may be treated as a no-show. No-shows may forfeit the full booking fee with no refund.

4.5 Exceptions

We may make exceptions to this policy for flight cancellations or significant delays, medical emergencies, or force majeure events (natural disasters, civil unrest, and similar circumstances beyond your control). We may ask for supporting documentation before granting an exception.

4.6 Changes to this policy

We may update this Cancellation & Refund Policy at any time. Changes take effect once published on this page or communicated through our official channels.

10. Emergency Button

RideArrivo provides an Emergency Button in the rider and driver apps and on this website. The button must only be used when an emergency occurs, meaning you or someone else in the vehicle faces a real threat to safety. You must not press it for complaints, disputes, or testing, and misuse may lead to suspension or removal from the service.

Pressing the Emergency Button alerts the RideArrivo operations team, who then activate the listening device in the vehicle so that they can hear what is happening and respond. By using the service, riders, drivers and vehicle owners acknowledge that the vehicles used for trips carry this device and that audio may be captured, listened to and kept in these circumstances only. The Emergency Button does not replace the emergency services: if you are in immediate danger, also call 112.

How the audio is used, who can access it, who it can be disclosed to, how long it is kept and how it is secured is set out in section 12 of our Privacy Policy, and in the driver agreement and the vehicle owner agreement.
